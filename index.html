Senior Consultant | Guide & Mentor | Trainer | Auditor

Prof. Dr. Krishna Arora is an orthodontist and educator with 18+ years of clinical and academic experience.

His professional journey combines orthodontic practice, university teaching, clinical leadership, digital orthodontics and structured mentoring. His approach is grounded in diagnosis, biomechanics, communication and evidence-informed treatment planning.
